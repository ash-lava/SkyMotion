SkyMotion 2.0.5 — validation, 15 November 2026 (UTC)

BASE AND PURPOSE
26c642d, the merged IRSA atlas repair, version 2.0.4.
The atlas now navigates freely beyond the selected target. The map center
and zoom are independent of the selected SPHEREx patch and cutout width.

BEHAVIOR
- A 6-degree surrounding view opens by default. Any valid decimal RA/Dec
  can be entered in the visible coordinate form, including celestial poles.
- Selecting coordinates or a preset retains the atlas zoom.
- All sky displays the complete sphere using Hammer-Aitoff projection.
  Local views up to 60 degrees retain TAN projection; wider values control
  overview zoom, with 360 fitting the full sphere on landscape or portrait.
- Drag, scroll/pinch, zoom buttons and keyboard controls navigate the map.
  RA wraps through zero. A selected target is a marker, not a map boundary.
- Switching views restores the last atlas position, zoom and grid state.
  Shared links save atlas_ra, atlas_dec and atlas_fov separately from the
  selected SPHEREx coordinates and 1-12 arcminute cutout width.
- Use this sky position selects the current center without resetting zoom.
  Suggested places are expandable; coordinates remain prominent in atlas mode.

GEOMETRY
The browser and server share TAN / inverse Hammer-Aitoff geometry. Wide-view
rotation handles both exact poles. Invalid pixels outside the all-sky ellipse
are masked before HEALPix lookup; they are rendered blank. TAN's LONPOLE is
explicitly 180 degrees so orientation also matches at the exact north pole.
Nearest-pixel sampling and the existing fixed-source/tile/memory bounds remain.

LIMITS AND DEPLOYMENT
Verified locally against live IRSA services, not after a Render deployment.
Survey plate boundaries and uneven backgrounds can be visible at whole-sky
scale. These are reference composites, not a live camera or calibrated
photometry. The atlas is reprojected after navigation rather than streaming
progressive tiles in the browser. External data-service failures remain
possible. SPHEREx still returns limited samples, not a complete survey census.

Merge the new PR, let Render deploy main, and confirm /api/health reports
2.0.5. No dependency or environment change is required relative to 2.0.4.
