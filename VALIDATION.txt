not a proxy-error page. It contains D3 and D6 observations. IRSA emits
unquoted JSON with commas in cloud_access; the parser repairs that declared
field only after validating its JSON, preserving later CSV columns.

failed at the proxy. Successful responses took about 21 seconds, longer than
the old 15-second SIA timeout. Identical small requests could also alternate
between success and 502. These facts support bounded requests, longer
waiting, and retries; they do not establish a single proxy failure cause or
a total archive outage. Detector filtering is now applied before MAXREC.

LIMITS
Live success is verified locally; it is not a claim that the deployed Render
service has already changed. IRSA failures remain intermittent. All five
challenge targets have not been verified end to end, and a successful live
TAP response is not claimed in this session; TAP has regression coverage.

Each SIA collection is limited to 16 records, or 4 after a smaller retry.
TAP is limited to 16. Limited counts are lower bounds, and an incomplete
sample may omit long time baselines. Four M101 exposures in the first test
span only six minutes. The broader browser result spans six days. Neither
is a complete M101 survey history.

FITS images retain the existing science limits: independent stretches,
varying center wavelengths, no PSF matching, and any-nonzero flag counts.
The M101 frames report nearly 100% of covered pixels with some flag bit set;
they are not represented as clean or calibrated variability measurements.
Read the exposure notes before interpreting apparent motion or brightness.

The CDS color atlas remains independent and optional. Its earlier live
failures were not repaired or presented as a successful atlas check.
